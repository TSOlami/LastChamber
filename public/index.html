      How it plays

      1. The counts are publicEach round the barker loads live and blank shells. Everyone sees how many of each. Nobody sees the order.
      2. Hold a target, let go to pullA live shell knocks that target down and costs it a life. A blank does nothing.
      3. Pull on yourself to gambleHit a blank on yourself and you keep the gun for another go. Hit a live one and it costs you.
      4. Items are optional tricksTap an item to read what it does, tap it again to use it. The last target standing takes the prize.

      You're out

      Your target stays down for the rest of the night. The rail plays on without you.
